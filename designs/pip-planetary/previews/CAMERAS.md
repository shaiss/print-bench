# pip-planetary — preview cameras

- **contact-sheet** — the standard 4-view sheet of the assembled print. The
  bottom-iso quadrant is the overhang/bed-contact check: the base annulus is
  the only bed contact, everything above prints on material.
- **gear-stratum** — camera at z=13 (mid-gear), low angle 30°, close: shows
  the toothed heart of the design — sun, three planets at 120°, and the
  internal ring teeth on the housing wall, all in mesh at once. This is the
  shot that shows the mechanism.
- **crank-top** — steep top-down view of the sun's molded crank arm and pin
  above the open housing top, showing how the input reaches the sun.
