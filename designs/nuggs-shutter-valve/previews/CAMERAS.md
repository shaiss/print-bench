# Preview cameras — nuggs-shutter-valve

Frozen cameras (see `cameras.conf`). Once a reviewer has seen a shot its camera
is fixed; a new region gets a new line, never a reframed existing one.

- **contact-sheet** — the 2×2 iso / top / front / bottom-iso auto sheet of the
  default (`cutaway`, closed) part.
- **hero** — three-quarter iso of the assembled valve CLOSED: the plate seals
  across the bore, handle/drawer to the near side, the open-topped park pocket
  visible. The shape a stranger sees first.
- **open** — same camera, gate fully OPEN (retracted into the pocket): the bore
  is clear top-to-bottom, the welfare non-negotiable — nothing left in the bore.
- **print-pose** — looks up from under the bed at the printable `valve` part
  (gate parked open over the pedestal). The view to scrutinise for printability:
  the 45° self-supporting skirt around the tube and the pedestal reaching the
  bed, so the wide drawer never faces the bed unsupported.
- **seal** — the `cutaway` part, sectioned, gate CLOSED: the plate spanning the
  bore is visible, confirming it seals the cross-section.
