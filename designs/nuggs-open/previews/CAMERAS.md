# Preview cameras — nuggs-open

Frozen cameras (see `cameras.conf`). Once a reviewer has seen a shot its camera
is fixed; a new region gets a new line, never a reframed existing one.

- **contact-sheet** — the 2×2 iso / top / front / bottom-iso auto sheet.
- **hero** — three-quarter iso from the window side (+X in the library frame):
  the shape a stranger sees first, the window's opening and its bore-arc floor
  both visible, both coupling rings in frame.
- **bottom-iso** — looks up from under the bed plane. The view to scrutinise
  for printability: the lower port's three sector-tip pads that form the only
  bed contact, and (past horizontal, rz=25) that no window surface faces down —
  the mouth planes are the only bridges and they print flat.
- **bore** — the `cutaway` part, sectioned on the y=0 plane through the
  window's crown, so the bore-arc floor running the full window with no
  interior ledge is visible end to end (charter N11, the welfare
  non-negotiable).
- **pair** — two modules coupled port-to-port, the run's-eye view: this part
  exists to sit inside a run, and the pair shows the interleaved sectors at the
  joint and the two windows clocked half a pitch apart. Review preview only —
  each module prints alone as the `open` part.
