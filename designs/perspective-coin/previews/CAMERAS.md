# Frozen preview cameras

- **contact-sheet** — the standard 4-view sheet of the default render (the
  print-in-place flipper charm): iso / top / front / bottom-iso.
- **face-a** — the bare coin straight from above: the JOY face ("COMPARISON
  IS THE THIEF OF JOY" arc legend, radiant sun, pivot diamonds at 3 and 9).
- **face-b** — the bare coin straight from below: the MEMENTO MORI face
  (arc legend, hourglass). Rendered from below, it reads **upright** — the two
  faces are in coin-flip alignment (a 180° rigid rotation about the pivot,
  det = +1, so it never mirrors). A slicer preview showing mirrored text means
  a mirror/flip transform is switched on, not the model.
- **charm** — the flipper charm at a high 3/4 angle: ring, keyring loop,
  moat, axle stubs, reeded coin edge.
- **cutaway** — the charm halved through the pivot axis (CGAL render,
  engraving off): the diamond axle sitting in its teardrop socket with the
  `pivot_clear` gap visible all around. QA shot for the print-in-place fit.
