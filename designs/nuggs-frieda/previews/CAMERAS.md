# nuggs-frieda — frozen preview cameras

- **contact-sheet** — the standard 4-view sheet (iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso). The bottom-iso quadrant is the overhang check: both cage
  skirts must read as cones, never as flat ledges.
- **word-front** — perspective on the R-I-E span at mid-word height: the
  letter relief over the inner tube, the base/cap rails, and both engraved
  mark bands.
- **word-back** — the same framing rotated 180°: the D-A end of the word and
  the plain rail span that closes the wrap (the word occupies ~289° of the
  circumference).
- **weld-fr** — orthographic close-up of the F→R junction, the load-bearing
  detail of the letter band: F's free arm ends must visibly FUSE into R's
  stem (they print as drooping cantilevers if a kern change re-opens the
  gap). Watches the same weld the letter_kern parameter exists for.
