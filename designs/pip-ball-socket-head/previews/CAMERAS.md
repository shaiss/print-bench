# Frozen preview cameras

| shot | what it shows |
|---|---|
| contact-sheet | the 4-view sheet (iso / top / front / bottom-iso) of the assembled default pose — bottom-iso is the overhang/bed-contact check |
| tilted-pose | the head tilted 15° about the ball centre (`part="head" demo_tilt=15`) — the articulation the `fitcheck_tilt` pose sweep proves on the mesh; the stud should sit centred in the dome aperture at this pose |
| collar-closeup | the capture band from the slit side: the rim throat and dome behind the clamp wings, with the slit between them — the fit the design exists to deliver, up close. Looking down the slit axis, the left pad can read as a detached capsule because the camera sees the 1.2 mm slot plus the cavity behind it; the wings are one body with the ring (fusecheck: 2 bodies on the sliced head) |
| collar-oblique | companion to collar-closeup, ~30° around Z from the slit: the pads' blend into the ring silhouette, with the rim and dome as the scale neighbour |
