# Preview cameras — sweetheart-hamster

Frozen shots driven by `cameras.conf` (rendered with `./scripts/render.sh
sweetheart-hamster --previews`). Cameras are fixed once seen — add a new line for
a new region, never move an existing one.

- **contact-sheet** — the default print pose, 4-view: both halves flat,
  cut-face-down on the bed, joined by the living-hinge web. This is what CI
  slices.
- **hero** — assembled (`fold=90`), 3/4 view: the chubby hamster cradling the
  heart, split seam down the middle. The product page's lead image.
- **front** — assembled, straight-on: the face (ears, eyes, muzzle) and the
  belly heart.
- **open-heart** — partway open (`fold=35`): the two halves splaying apart about
  the dorsal spine, the heart parting between them — the reveal.
