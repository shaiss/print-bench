# Frozen preview cameras — nuggs-turnaround

What each committed shot in this directory shows, so a reviewer knows where to
look before reacting. Cameras are fixed once a reviewer has seen a shot; a new
region gets a new `cameras.conf` line, never a moved one.

- **contact-sheet.png** — the repo-standard 2×2 sheet (iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso). The **bottom-iso** quadrant is the overhang and bed-contact
  check: both port rings stand on their sector tips, the solid web spans them
  on the bed, and the belly dome touches down pole-flush at the centre with
  nothing hanging below the port plane.
- **hero.png** — three-quarter exterior of the whole node: both ports down on
  their sector tips, the solid web between them, the oblate chamber rising to
  the crown, the vent row on the use-ceiling flank.
- **cutaway.png** — section on the plane through both port axes (the print
  x–z plane at y = 0 is *not* this plane; this one contains the two bore axes
  at y = ±48.5). Shows both vertical Ø80 bores, the solid web between them
  below, and the dish floor handing off to each bore mouth ~1 mm **below** the
  bore floor — the no-lip step the route takes. Viewed from the −x
  (use-ceiling) flank, so the vents on that flank are in the near wall.
- **cutaway-cross.png** — section **across** the turn axis at y = 0, the
  chamber's own profile, viewed slightly elevated for depth. The ceiling from
  z = 140 up is the **barrel vault**: two flat roof planes at ~46° meeting at
  a ridge line (z = 181.4 at x = 0) over the dish floor — the crown cap that
  took the printcheck overhang figure from 9% to 2% of surface. Note when
  reading it: the *outer* shell above the vault is the part's own dome (pole
  at z = 184.8, 3.4 mm over the ridge), and from this camera's shallow
  elevation the two curves nearly coincide — the gable is the interior line
  just under the silhouette. `cutaway-profile` is the unambiguous version.
- **cutaway-profile.png** — the same y = 0 section **dead-on**, no elevation:
  the profile reads as a drawing, and the vault's two straight roof planes and
  the ridge corner at the top of the cavity are not hidden behind the outer
  dome's silhouette. This is the shot that shows the crown-cap decision
  directly.
