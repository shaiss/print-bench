# Preview cameras — nuggs-elbow

Frozen cameras (see `cameras.conf`). Once a reviewer has seen a shot its camera
is fixed; a new region gets a new line, never a reframed existing one.

- **contact-sheet** — the 2×2 iso / top / front / bottom-iso auto sheet.
- **hero** — three-quarter iso, inlet flange down and the outlet port at 45°;
  the shape a stranger sees first, bore visible through the outlet.
- **bottom-iso** — looks up from under the bed plane. The view to scrutinise for
  printability: the inlet-port sector tips that form the (small) bed-contact
  ring, and the gentle underside overhang of the bend belly.
- **bore** — the `cutaway` part, sectioned on the bend plane, so the continuous
  smooth interior wall through the corner is visible end to end (the welfare
  non-negotiable: no interior ledge or step).
