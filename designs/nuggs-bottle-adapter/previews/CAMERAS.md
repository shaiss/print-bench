# Camera descriptions — nuggs-bottle-adapter

| Shot | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `contact-sheet` | The repo-standard 4-view sheet (iso / top / front / bottom-iso). The bottom-iso quadrant is the overhang/bed-contact check: sector tips down, no supports. |
| `hero` | Three-quarter of the whole part in the print pose: coupling ring and sector-tip feet at the bed, the shoulder cone closing Ø84.8 down to the ~Ø34 throat, the threaded mouth at the top. |
| `cutaway` | Half-section on the axial plane: the 80 mm port bore opening into the 44° interior funnel, the land annulus where the bottle's lip seats, the female thread grooves, and the 45° rim lead-in. |
| `cutaway-profile` | The same section dead-on, profile-as-drawing: bore → funnel ceiling → land → groove band → rim, with the thread pitch visible as the groove spacing. |
| `throat` | Oblique (~40° off-axis) full-render into the mouth: the 45° rim lead-in flare, then the thread groove band crossing the visible bore wall as four light/dark ridge arcs (the 2.7 mm pitch projected), the bright land annulus, and the 22 mm opening into the shadowed funnel. Rendered at `$fn=192` — at this close range the production 96-gon rim shows ~1.1 mm chord flats. |
| `bed-contact` | Low tight three-quarter on one castellated sector-tip foot — the only bed contact, the gaps between tips, and the shell ring bridging 10 mm above the bed where first-layer islands merge. |
