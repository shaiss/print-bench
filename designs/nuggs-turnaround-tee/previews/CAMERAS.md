# Frozen preview cameras — nuggs-turnaround-tee

What each committed shot in this directory shows, so a reviewer knows where to
look before reacting. Cameras are fixed once a reviewer has seen a shot; a new
region gets a new `cameras.conf` line, never a moved one. The camera set is the
parent's (same conventions, center raised to the tee's mid-height) — the tee is
a derivative, so its previews read like the parent's with one more feature in
each frame.

- **contact-sheet.png** — the repo-standard 2×2 sheet (iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso). The **bottom-iso** quadrant is the overhang and bed-contact
  check: both bed-port rings stand on their sector tips — still the only bed
  contact — the web bridges between them at the port plane (10 mm above the
  bed, not on it), and the belly dome closes pole-flush at that same plane.
  Port C prints **mouth-up** on the crown; nothing about the bed picture
  changes from the parent's.
- **hero.png** — three-quarter exterior of the whole node: both bed ports down
  on their sector tips, the solid web between them, the oblate chamber rising
  to the crown, and port C's collar + sector ring uppermost, offset 3 mm toward
  the dish-floor flank.
- **cutaway.png** — section on the plane through both bed-port axes (y = ±48.5):
  both vertical Ø80 bores, the solid web between them below, the dish floor
  handing off to each bore mouth, and port C's neck-tube walls (the plane runs
  3 mm beside C's axis) rising through the frame to its ring. Viewed from the
  −x (use-ceiling) flank, so the vents on that flank are in the near wall.
- **cutaway-cross.png** — section **across** the turn axis at y = 0 — the plane
  that carries the whole A→C through-route: the dish floor rising through the
  frame to the handoff at z = 120.3, where port C's flat bore floor takes over
  at 13.9°, and the repaired crown cap over it. Port C's collar is cut
  lengthwise — a half-tube channel opening to the camera at the top of the
  frame; read the bore as continuing down into the cavity. Note when reading
  it: the *outer* shell above the roof is the part's own dome silhouette, and
  from this camera's shallow elevation the interior roof lines sit just under
  it — `cutaway-profile` is the unambiguous version.
- **cutaway-profile.png** — the same y = 0 section **dead-on**, no elevation:
  the profile reads as a drawing. The cavity ceiling is the repaired cap: two
  straight ≥45° **gable** planes meeting the ridge corner (z = 168.2 at x = 0)
  at the top of the frame, with the steeper **hip** descent projected behind
  them where the roof turns down toward the y-ends. The roof is closed
  everywhere — the parent's crown through-slot (follow-up issue on the parent)
  is what this cap exists to prevent, and the shell keeps ≥ 2.7 mm over the
  cavity at every sampled point. This is the shot that shows the cap-repair
  decision directly.
- **bed-contact.png** — low, tight three-quarter on one bed port's castellated
  sector-tip feet, the part's **only** bed contact: the tips, the gaps between
  them, and a slice of the neighbouring ring fill the frame, with the
  collar/web plane bridging 10 mm above the bed. Same story as the parent's —
  the first ~50 layers print as twelve separate islands (these feet) that merge
  at the port plane, and a brim would weld across them.
