# Preview cameras — nuggs-y-splitter

Frozen cameras (see `cameras.conf`). Once a reviewer has seen a shot its camera
is fixed; a new region gets a new line, never a reframed existing one.

- **contact-sheet** — the 2×2 iso / top / front / bottom-iso auto sheet.
- **hero** — three-quarter iso, inlet port down and both branch ports rising at
  30°; the shape a stranger sees first, the bores visible through the outlets.
- **bottom-iso** — looks up from under the bed plane. The view to scrutinise
  for printability: the inlet-port sector tips that form the (small)
  bed-contact ring, and the 30°-from-vertical underside of the fork wedge.
- **bore** — the `cutaway` part, sectioned on the fork plane, so the continuous
  smooth interior wall through the junction is visible in all three arms (the
  welfare non-negotiable: no interior ledge or step; the blend sphere swallows
  every cavity cap).
