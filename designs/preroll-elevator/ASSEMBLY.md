# Pre-roll elevator — 5 printed parts, no hardware — assembly

![Exploded view](previews/exploded.png)

## Bill of materials

| Part | Qty | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `screw_knob()` | 1 | central lead-screw + hex twist knob (the "bolt"); prints hex-head down |
| `retainer()` | 1 | base capture ring; press-snaps into the body base, traps the screw flange |
| `body()` | 1 | threaded bolt-shank tube with 4 anti-rotation slots; prints base down |
| `elevator()` | 1 | carrier nut + 4 roll cups + 4 anti-rotation tabs; prints cups up |
| `lid()` | 1 | hex cap-nut lid; prints closed-top down |

## Assembly steps

1. Insert the screw-knob up through the body from below — the journal passes through the floor bore and the flange's 45° cone seats on the matching countersink.
2. From inside the body, drop the retainer ring down over the screw shaft and press it into the base counterbore until it clicks flush; the screw now spins freely but cannot lift out.
3. Thread the elevator onto the screw from the open top, aligning its 4 tabs with the 4 body slots, and wind it down until the tabs run in the slots.
4. Drop one pre-roll into each of the 4 cups.
5. Twist the knob to lower the rolls fully inside, then screw the hex cap-nut lid onto the top to close.
