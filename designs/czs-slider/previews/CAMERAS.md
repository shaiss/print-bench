# Camera descriptions

Per-shot descriptions for `cameras.conf` (the freeze convention: a camera is
fixed once reviewed; a new region gets a new line).

- **contact-sheet** — the production part, 2×2 iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso. The bottom-iso is the print-orientation proof: everything
  stands on the bed, the only bridge is the capture deck.
- **qzs-top** — straight down over the whole part: the guided slot and knob
  at left, the spring chamber at right. Shows the parallel pair — the
  pre-buckled arch bowing −X from its chord line and the V-beam chevron
  opening +X — anchored between the same head and far bar, and the chamber
  walls standing clear of both sweeps.
- **qzs-midspan** — close, near-top, on the chamber's mid-span: the arch
  band and the V legs crossing paths without touching, and the 0.6 mm sweep
  clearances to the chamber walls.
- **coupon** — the print-this-first strip, straight down along its long
  axis: fit cells 1/2/3 (k_xy = 0.40/0.45/0.50, production = 2) with their
  digits cut through the near end walls (small at strip scale — zoom to
  read; verified legible magnified), then the QZS feeler cell (rotated,
  chamber at right).
