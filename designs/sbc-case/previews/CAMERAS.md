# Preview shots (frozen cameras)

| shot | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `iso` | assembled case with the Raspberry Pi 4 vitamin inside and the 40 mm fan on the lid — the overall shape a reviewer reacts to first |
| `top` | straight down: fan aperture and bosses, lid-screw positions, the lid plate outline |
| `ports` | low angle on the +X edge: USB-A / Ethernet cable clearance through the open skirt, the webbed vent row |
| `board` | base tray alone from the −Y/above: the four standoffs generated from `pcb_screw_positions(RPI4)`, the through-bored lid-screw posts, skirt profile |
| `notch` | bare tray with the Pi seated, three-quarter from +Y: the GPIO access notch cut down from the +Y wall top edge, the 2×20 header reaching through it (feature #4 — jumper access with the lid on), the webbed vent row below |
