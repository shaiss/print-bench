# Frozen preview cameras — nuggs-sand-sieve

What each committed shot in this directory shows, so a reviewer knows where to
look before reacting. Cameras are fixed once a reviewer has seen a shot; a new
region gets a new `cameras.conf` line, never a moved one.

- **contact-sheet.png** — the repo-standard 2×2 sheet (iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso). The **bottom-iso** quadrant is the overhang and bed-contact
  check: the port ring stands on its three sector tips — the only bed contact —
  and the flare climbs at ≤45° from the tube OD to the basket.
- **hero.png** — three-quarter exterior of the whole sieve: coupling ring down
  on its sector tips, the flare into the open basket, mouth up with the
  chamfered pour lip.
- **grid.png** — straight down into the basket: the orthogonal web lattice at
  production `hole_d` / `web_w` across the full floor.
- **cutaway.png** — section on the port-axis plane: the port zone, the flare
  draining into the bore, the grid floor, the basket wall and the 45° pour lip.
