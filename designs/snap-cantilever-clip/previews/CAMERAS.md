# Preview shot descriptions

What each frozen `cameras.conf` shot exists to show. Cameras are frozen
once reviewed — add a line, never reframe one.

- **contact-sheet** — the toolchain's fixed 2×2 (iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso) of the default render. The bottom-iso quadrant is the
  overhang / bed-contact check: the part is a flat-extruded silhouette,
  so every wall is vertical and the whole bed face lands at once.
- **iso** — the whole clip 3/4 from the mouth corner: the channel
  opening, the stiff jaw with its funnel chamfer, the guard rail on the
  far side, the crown strap closing the far end. The "what is this
  object" shot.
- **profile** — orthographic top view (+Z, the bed plane): the design's
  working drawing. Channel, 3.2 mm gap, the lip tooth with its 30°
  lead-in ramp and 45° retention shoulder, the blunt 0.8 mm apex, the
  guard gap, the root fillet arc — every angle in the design readable in
  one shot. This is also the flexure-root fillet's record: the fillet is
  an interior corner with no robust camera corridor (a close-up either
  occludes behind the lip or looks edge-on down the channel), so its
  geometry is documented here instead.
- **mouth** — looking into the mouth and down the channel: the throat
  the plate must pass (1.8 mm), the lead-in funnel on the stiff jaw, the
  lip tooth dead centre with both cam faces. The insertion story.
- **root** — the flexure root from the guard side, elevated: the guard
  rail, the guard-gap slot (open to the top face, so it reads as a dark
  groove), the finger's flat back across the slot, and the crown strap
  converging on the root corner where all three join.
- **root-detail** — the close cut root's wider frame could not deliver
  (added per review; `root` stays frozen): looking down over the crown
  strap from above the guard-side rear corner, the guard-gap slot and the
  channel terminate against the strap in the foreground — the root-fillet
  arc rounds the channel's corner, the finger's flat back stands between
  the two dark grooves, and the guard rail edges the near side.
