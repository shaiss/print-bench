# Camera descriptions

Per-shot descriptions for `cameras.conf` (the freeze convention: a camera is
fixed once reviewed; a new region gets a new line).

- **contact-sheet** — the assembled short rail with its first three ghost
  bits, 2×2 iso / top / front / bottom-iso. The bottom-iso is the
  print-orientation proof: full flat bed contact, no supports, pockets up.
- **rail-short** — the printable short rail alone, 3/4 ortho down its length:
  twelve hex pockets in one row centred on the 40 mm width (equal wall either
  side), each mouth broken 45° for one-handed insertion.
- **rail-long** — the printable long rail, same framing: the identical
  vocabulary at the 18 mm deep socket for 50/75 mm bits.
- **coupon** — the print-this-first strip, near-top ortho so the six embossed
  fit values read left to right: **-0.15 -0.15 0 0 +0.15 +0.15** across
  flats. This is the shot to compare a feel-test against — the pocket that
  clicks in and releases one-handed names the value to print the rail at.
- **cutaway** — front section through the pocket axis: pocket depth against
  the seated ghost bit, the 2.4 mm floor, and the 45° mouth break — the fit
  you cannot see from outside. (Full render: the section is a difference of
  the ghost-bit assembly.)
- **cutaway-close** — the same section at the first three pockets, pure front
  view. Added before the first review (a new line, the full-length shot stays
  frozen): at rail framing the 14.4 mm section rendered 70 px tall — the
  close-up is the one a reader can actually read depths off.
