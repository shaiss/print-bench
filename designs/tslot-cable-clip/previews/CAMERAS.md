# Camera descriptions — tslot-cable-clip

Rendered by `./scripts/render.sh tslot-cable-clip --previews`. Cameras are
frozen once reviewed: a new region to show gets a NEW line in
`cameras.conf`, never a moved camera (before/after comparisons across
review rounds must align).

- **contact-sheet** — the 2×2 iso / top / front / bottom-iso of the default
  render (the three-station lineup). The bottom-iso is the print-pose
  check: every wall vertical, three bodies flat on the bed.
- **family** — the three v1 stations in a row (A Ø4 / B Ø6.2 / C Ø8),
  mouths facing +x, the lineup exactly as the default render draws it.
- **installed** — station B seated in a short E2020 rail section (the
  fitcheck pair posed together): hammer-head lug in the side slot, body
  standing on the extrusion face, loop routing along the rail axis.
- **mouth** — orthographic close-up of station B's entry side: the 45°
  funnel, the throat between the two flex tabs, and the thinned front
  sector (the tab wall `tab_t` thick vs the 2.4 back).
- **fin** — orthographic back-iso of station B: the hammer-head lug fin
  (neck 5.7 through the 6.0 mouth, head 10.4 hooking 2.2 per side behind
  the lips) standing on the bed against the seating face.
- **coupon** — the print-this-first coupon, high-angle ortho plan (strip laid
  across the frame): the production lug's hammer-head profile on its back strip
  (left) and the three production loop sections sweeping `cable_fit`
  0.0 / 0.2 / 0.4 (right), mouths and 45° funnels readable in plan.
