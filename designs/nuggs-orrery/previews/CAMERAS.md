# nuggs-orrery — preview cameras

- **contact-sheet** — the standard 4-view sheet of the default render
  (`part="assembled"`, the co-print state with the sprue frame). The
  bottom-iso quadrant is the overhang/bed-contact check.
- **ring-station** — close three-quarter view centered on the middle ring
  station (`part="kinetic"`, rings seated, sprues snapped): shows the ring
  riding its conical race inside the groove, the fin cutbacks above it, and
  the captivity overlap. This is the shot that shows the mechanism.
