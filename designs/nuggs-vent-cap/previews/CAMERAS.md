# Frozen preview cameras — nuggs-vent-cap

What each committed shot in this directory shows, so a reviewer knows where to
look before reacting. Cameras are fixed once a reviewer has seen a shot; a new
region gets a new `cameras.conf` line, never a moved one.

- **contact-sheet.png** — the repo-standard 2×2 sheet (iso / top / front /
  bottom-iso). The **bottom-iso** quadrant is the overhang and bed-contact
  check: the port ring stands on its three sector tips — the only bed contact
  — the collar plane bridges ~10 mm above the bed, and the lattice cone rises
  from the wall rim with nothing printing over void except the named bridges
  (strand undersides, crown spokes).
- **hero.png** — three-quarter exterior of the whole cap: the coupling ring
  down on its sector tips, the full-round shell, and the 45° lattice cone
  rising to the flat latticed crown.
- **lattice-top.png** — straight down into the dome: the crown disc (15 radial
  spokes, concentric bands, the natural hub where the spokes cross) and the 37
  meridian ribs falling away to the wall rim. This is the shot that shows the
  on-surface cell openings the `aperture_max` welfare ceiling governs.
- **cells.png** — tight three-quarter on the rim cells: the 1.2 mm strands and
  the ≤ `aperture_max` openings at the scale they actually print.
- **cutaway.png** — section on the port-axis plane, dead-on (no elevation, so
  the 45° lines read as lines): the port zone from the sector tips to the
  collar, the dome's seating ring buried in the wall band **below** `z_top`,
  the lattice falling off the wall at exactly 45°, and the crown disc bridging
  the last 12 mm of radius.
